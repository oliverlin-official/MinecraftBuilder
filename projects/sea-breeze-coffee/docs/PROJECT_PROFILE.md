# PROJECT_PROFILE — Sea Breeze Coffee

## Classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Project name | Sea Breeze Coffee |

## Brief

Build a cozy seaside coffee shop matching the panorama reference: warm plaster walls, dark timber vaulted beams, open arched ocean views, blue accent doors/seating, service counter with menu board, and plant-heavy coastal decor.

## Primary concerns (PROFILE_A)

- Massing and proportion of the open-front pavilion
- Minecraft-native detail (beams, arches, counter, seating)
- Interior accessibility (doorways, aisle clearance)
- Block palette from reference
- Roof / vaulted ceiling grammar
- Facade rhythm toward the sea

## Must-have spaces

1. Service counter + espresso / pastry display zone
2. Open dining floor with wooden tables and blue-cushion chairs
3. Window-bar seating facing the ocean
4. Dual blue entrance doors (left / right of panorama)
5. Open arched seaside openings with exterior railing
6. Signage: "Sea Breeze Coffee" + chalkboard menu + outdoor directional post

## Optional / later polish

- Ceiling vines and hanging plants
- Blue branded rugs
- Sailboats / distant coastal hills (landscape context, not core architecture)
- Ceiling fan / pendant lighting density

## Explicitly out of scope (v1)

- Full settlement / district planning
- Road network generation
- Large terraform beyond light coastal adaptation at the chosen footprint
