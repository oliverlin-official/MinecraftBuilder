# STYLE_BIBLE — Sea Breeze Coffee

## Mood

Warm, airy, coastal café. Soft daylight, open ocean vista, lived-in wood, blue accents, abundant greenery.

## Architecture

- Semi-open pavilion / curved or wide open front toward beach
- Cream / off-white plaster walls
- Heavy dark timber posts, curved vault ribs, plank ceiling
- Large arched seaside openings (minimal glazing; view-first)
- Blue painted wooden doors with glass panes
- Light polished wood floor following room curve
- Exterior low wooden railing + weathered directional signpost

## Scale notes

- Player clearance: doorways ≥ 2 blocks high; aisles ≥ 2 blocks wide
- Counter height: 1–1.5 block working surface with stool/customer side
- Open arches: tall enough for clear ocean vista (prefer 3–4 block clear height)
- Building footprint target (draft): ~18–28 blocks wide × 12–18 deep (adjust after site analysis)

## Do / Don't

- Do keep the seaside face highly open
- Do concentrate detail on counter, seating, plants, and lighting
- Don't fill arches with solid walls or dense glass that kills the vista
- Don't flatten a large beach just to place a box; adapt massing to the coast
